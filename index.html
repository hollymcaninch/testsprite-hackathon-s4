01 · Open an issue

Say what is broken

Describe the problem and assign the issue to yourself, so everyone can see what's already being worked on. PRs titled docs: skip this step.

Up to three open claimed issues at a time. A claim with no activity gets a warning at 48 hours and is released at 72, so nobody sits on work they aren't doing.

02 · Open a PR

Fix it and tick the box

Your PR body needs Closes #123 pointing at your issue, the entry checkbox ticked, and your Discord username so we can reach you. Not on Discord? We'll reach you on the PR.

A bot confirms your entry in a comment. If it looks like an entry but the box is unticked, it asks rather than excludes you. Up to three bonus-eligible PRs per person; contribute as much as you like beyond that.

Two bars, then four aspects

A PR has to be merged and judged a genuine improvement. Those are separate bars, so a PR can land and still earn nothing. Agents screen every entry; a human decides the payouts. Each eligible PR is then read against four aspects, with a "meet" and an "exceed" for each. Up to two revision rounds on substance.

Impact

What does it do for a real user?

Meet: fixes a reproducible bug or adds a capability people asked for. Exceed: closes a class of bug, ships a product-clarity feature, or sets a policy the repo then follows.

Problem definition

Is the problem real?

Meet: the problem is real and someone else could reproduce it from what you wrote. Exceed: a problem we didn't know we had, or one that reaches further than the fix.

Craft

Does it merge clean?

Meet: tests included, CI green on every platform, diff matches the title. Exceed: right on the first review round with no collateral.

Discipline

Does it help others land theirs?

Meet: problem stated before code, one concern per PR, credit given to whoever reported it. Exceed: reshapes fast on a maintainer's redirect, or files work another contributor picks up and ships.

Fair play

Losing a duplicate race costs nothing: the first to file keeps that credit and the author of the merged PR keeps theirs. A deadline for allocation is not a quality mark, so a fix that lands just after the window still counts as the work it is. Maintainer-side incidents never count against you.

What does not qualify

These get merged or closed on their own merits, they just don't earn a payout. The list is illustrative, not exhaustive. Bounty farming ends your eligibility for the season.

No problem statement Reformatting, whitespace, typo-level edits Behavior change with no tests Comment-only or no-context edits Unexplained dependency bumps One change split across several PRs Spam or bounty farming

Feedback

Tell Us What's Broken.

Open all season · every surface

You're using it harder than we do.

Two weeks of real builds will surface things we've missed. If something in the MCP server, the 3.0 web portal, or the CLI got in your way, confused you, or just felt wrong, tell us. Specific beats polite: what you tried, what happened, what you expected.

Share feedback in Discord

MCP Server

Tool descriptions that misled your agent, calls that failed silently, setup steps that took too long.

Web Portal

Test generation that missed the point, verdicts that were hard to read, anything you had to work around in 3.0.

CLI

Flags, output, docs, install. If it's a bug with a fix, open the issue and PR instead, and it can count toward the bonus.

Credits for the good stuff. Feedback that changes what we build may earn extra TestSprite credits as a thank-you. It's not a guarantee and it isn't scored, it's how we'd like to say thanks for helping make the product better.
